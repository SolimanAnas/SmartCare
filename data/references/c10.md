## 10.1 Airway and breathing

- Suction no longer than 15 seconds in adults, 10 seconds in children,
  5 seconds in infants. OPA sized corner of mouth to earlobe; adults
  inserted upside down and rotated 180°. NPA nostril to earlobe; caution in
  basal skull fracture. (NREMT)
- Ventilation: breaths over 1 second; adults 1 every 6 seconds; children and
  infants 1 every 2–3 seconds; 30:2 adult CPR, 15:2 child with two
  rescuers. (AHA 2025)
- Oxygen devices: nasal cannula 1–6 L/min, 24–44%; simple mask
  6–10 L/min, 35–55%; non-rebreather 12–15 L/min, 60–90%; bag-mask with
  reservoir at 15 L/min close to 100%. Targets 94–98%, COPD 88–92% (an
  SpO₂ of 99% is too high for a CO₂ retainer). (NREMT, BTS)

## 10.2 Oxygen and ventilation

- Cylinder minutes = (pressure − 200 psi) × factor ÷ flow; factors D 0.16,
  E 0.28, M 1.56, H 3.14; full at about 2000 psi. Examples:
  (2000 − 200) × 0.28 ÷ 15 ≈ 34 minutes; (1600 − 200) × 0.28 ÷ 15 ≈ 26;
  (1000 − 200) × 0.28 ÷ 15 ≈ 15 minutes. (NREMT)
- ETCO₂ normal 35–45 mmHg; above 45 mmHg hypoventilation; below 35 mmHg
  hyperventilation or low output; below 10 mmHg in CPR means poor
  compressions; sudden rise suggests ROSC. (AHA 2025)
- Methaemoglobinaemia gives SpO₂ around 85%. Assist ventilation if
  respiratory rate is below 8/min or breathing inadequate. (NREMT)

## 10.3 Bleeding control and wound care

- Tourniquet 5–7 cm (2–3 inches) above the wound, not over a joint;
  forearm and lower-leg tourniquets are effective; second tourniquet
  above the first; never loosen in the field. Wound packing with at least
  3 minutes of pressure. Pelvic binder at the greater trochanters.
  (CoTCCC, Stop the Bleed, ATLS)
- A femur fracture can lose 1–1.5 L of blood. (ATLS)

## 10.4 and 10.5 Splinting and spinal motion restriction

- Traction splints only for isolated mid-shaft femur fractures. (NREMT)
- SMR 2018 indications; penetrating trauma without deficit needs no
  immobilisation; NEXUS criteria; log roll with at least 4 people; children
  under about 8 need shoulder padding; pregnancy over 20 weeks: left tilt.
  Age 65 or older is a Canadian C-spine rule factor, not a NEXUS criterion.
  (SMR 2018, NEXUS)

## 10.6 Patient movement and lifting

- Power grip with hands about 25 cm apart; bariatric moves with 4–6 or more
  rescuers. (NREMT)
- Landing zone at least about 30 × 30 m (100 × 100 feet); approach from the
  front when signalled, never from the tail or uphill side. (AAMS, FAA)

## 10.7 12-lead ECG

- 12-lead within 10 minutes of first contact. V1 and V2 in the 4th
  intercostal space (angle of Louis = 2nd rib); V4 5th intercostal space
  mid-clavicular; commonest error V1/V2 in the 2nd or 3rd space. V4R right
  5th space; V7–V9 posterior at the level of V6. Mains interference 50 or
  60 Hz. (AHA, ISCE)

## 10.8 Medication administration

- Nitroglycerin: not if SBP below 90 mmHg or 30 below baseline, HR below 50
  or above 100, RV infarction, sildenafil or vardenafil within 24 hours,
  tadalafil within 48 hours. Aspirin 162–325 mg chewed. (AHA)
- Epinephrine auto-injector 0.3 mg; 0.15 mg under 25 kg. Pediatric
  naloxone 0.1 mg/kg, maximum 2 mg (14 kg → 1.4 mg = 1.4 mL of 1 mg/mL).
  Albuterol 2.5 mg nebulised; nebuliser flow 6–8 L/min; intranasal about 1 mL per nostril at most. (PALS, NREMT)
- Chapter 10 study questions use AHA 2025 values throughout.
