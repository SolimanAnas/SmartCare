# Chapter images: to be generated

Illustrations planned for the rewritten chapter pages. They are not on the
pages yet, and the pages carry no placeholders. When an image is made, add it
to the topic's guide in `content/cN.js` at the place named below.

Each entry gives:

- **Where**: topic id, then the guide section the image goes in (by its title)
  and whether it goes before or after that section's content.
- **Image**: what it shows.
- **Notes**: style and accuracy points.

House style for every image:

- flat, clean medical illustration, not photographic;
- transparent or plain light background, so it reads in all five app themes
  (dark, AMOLED, light, sepia, forest);
- the chapter accent color as the highlight color;
- short labels only, in plain sans-serif, large enough to read at 360 px wide;
- neutral, diverse patients and rescuers; gloves on when touching a patient;
- landscape about 4:3, or square for single-object diagrams;
- SVG or WebP, under 150 KB each.
