    Key distinction

    Educational visualisation only. These respiratory waveforms are conceptual representations generated for learning and pattern recognition. They are not monitor traces, not physiological simulations, and not a substitute for patient assessment, monitoring, clinical judgement or local protocols. Amplitude is a dimensionless chest excursion, not a measured volume or pressure.

    Further reading on respiratory patterns is available from established clinical education sources including the American Heart Association, the Merck Manual, StatPearls / NCBI Bookshelf, and standard EMS and nursing assessment textbooks. Always follow your own service's clinical guidelines and local protocols for patient care.
